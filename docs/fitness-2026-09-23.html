髖關節發育不良患者接受髖臼切骨術併或不併股骨頭頸部骨塑術的 10 年長期追蹤

The American journal of sports medicine · Periacetabular Osteotomy and Combined Open Femoral Head-Neck Junction Osteochondroplasty: A Concise Follow-up of a Previous Report at a Mean 10-Year Follow-up.
